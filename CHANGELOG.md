# Changelog

## 0.1 - 2026-11-10

First release.

- Every new character spawns as one of 22 roles - police officer, doctor, nurse, paramedic,
  soldier, technician, hunter, motorcyclist, firefighter, lumberjack, prisoner, journalist,
  athlete, executive, NBC specialist, tradesman, medieval re-enactor, sniper, tank crew,
  military police, pilot and hiker - in the matching vanilla outfit with a few things that
  fit the job, and is told who they are ("You are a Firefighter").
- Server-side only: players download nothing.
- The roles are a JSON file the server writes on its first start,
  `profiles/DayZOfOurLives/Roles.json`: change outfits, items and how often each role comes
  up, or add your own.
